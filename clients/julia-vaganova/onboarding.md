# Julia Vaganova - Portfolio Platform Onboarding

**Date:** 2026-06-25  
**Client:** Julia Vaganova  
**Workstream:** MoteOps client onboarding  
**Project:** Professional artistic portfolio, modeling portfolio, and creative showcase platform

## Brief

Julia wants to work on a professional artistic portfolio and modeling-focused creative platform. The platform should showcase her modeling portfolio, strengthen her creative presence, and help her gain more exposure.

## Goals

- Present Julia's modeling work in a polished, professional format.
- Communicate her artistic identity and range.
- Make it easy for collaborators, agencies, photographers, stylists, casting teams, and brands to understand her work.
- Create a platform that can be shared publicly and expanded over time.

## Suggested Audience Segments

- Modeling agencies and scouts
- Photographers and creative directors
- Stylists, makeup artists, and production collaborators
- Casting professionals
- Brands and editorial teams
- Fans / general audience discovering Julia's work

## Suggested Platform Structure

1. **Home / Landing**
   - Strong hero image or editorial montage
   - Short positioning statement
   - Clear calls to view portfolio and contact/book

2. **Portfolio**
   - Gallery organized by category, shoot, mood, campaign, or medium
   - Support for high-quality images and possibly video/reels
   - Credits for photographers, stylists, makeup artists, publications, and collaborators

3. **About**
   - Short professional bio
   - Artist/modeling statement
   - Location/availability if Julia wants it public

4. **Experience / Credits**
   - Campaigns, publications, runway, collaborations, exhibitions, or notable projects

5. **Contact / Booking**
   - Inquiry form or protected email flow
   - Social links
   - Optional downloadable comp card / media kit

## Discovery Questions

### Brand & Identity
- Should the site be branded as **Julia Vaganova**, an artist alias, or both?
- What adjectives should define the visual direction: editorial, cinematic, minimal, bold, luxury, avant-garde, natural, commercial, experimental?
- Are there existing colors, fonts, logos, or references she loves?

### Portfolio Content
- How many images are ready to publish?
- Are images already edited and cleared for web use?
- Should work be grouped by photoshoot, genre, campaign, or mood?
- Are there videos, reels, behind-the-scenes clips, interviews, or press links?

### Professional Goals
- Is the primary goal bookings, agency visibility, artistic credibility, social growth, press, or all of these?
- Which audiences are most important in the next 3-6 months?
- What actions should visitors take after viewing the site?

### Operations
- Does Julia already have a domain?
- Who will update the site after launch?
- Should the platform include a CMS for easy content updates?
- Are there privacy or safety requirements for contact info, location, or image access?

## Recommended MVP

A focused first version should include:
- Homepage
- Portfolio gallery
- About page
- Contact/booking page
- Social links
- Basic SEO and share metadata
- Mobile-first responsive design
